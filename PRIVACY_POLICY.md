# Privacy Policy for Orbit Breaker

**Last updated:** November 4, 2026

**Orbit Breaker** ("we", "our", or "the Game"), developed by Dreamcall, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you download, install, and play Orbit Breaker on mobile devices.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

- **No Personal Account Required:** Orbit Breaker does not require user registration, email sign-ins, phone numbers, or passwords.
- **Local Gameplay Data Only:** Your game progress, unlocked cosmic sectors, high scores, collected coins, and audio preferences are stored exclusively on your local device via local storage. We do not transmit or store your personal gameplay records on external private databases.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Orbit Breaker integrates **Google AdMob** (a service provided by Google LLC) to serve optional in-game rewarded advertisements (such as reviving a run or continuing gameplay).

### What Google AdMob May Collect:
Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information automatically, including:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (GAID/IDFA), device model, operating system version, and system language.
- **Network & Diagnostic Data:** IP address (used to infer coarse country/region location for localized ads), network provider, and general performance logs.
- **Ad Interaction Data:** Views, clicks, and impressions to provide relevant ads and detect click fraud.

You can learn more about how Google processes and protects advertising data by reviewing the [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

## 3. Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You can reset your device's advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings:
- **Android:** Go to *Settings* -> *Google* -> *Ads* (or *Privacy* -> *Ads*) -> Tap *Delete advertising ID* or toggle *Opt out of Ads Personalization*.
- **iOS:** Go to *Settings* -> *Privacy & Security* -> *Tracking* -> Toggle off tracking permissions for Orbit Breaker.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Orbit Breaker is designed for general audiences and arcade gaming enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove any associated data.

---

## 5. Security of Your Information

Because all game progress is saved locally on your device, your data security is primarily tied to your device's local security protections. We implement standard security practices within our code to protect the integrity of the application.

---

- The right to know whether personal data is processed.

- The right to request deletion of any collected analytical data.
